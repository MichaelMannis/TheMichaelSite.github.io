ROVER

Overview

Entry for the Olympus Rover Trials
This is a project as part of StrathSEDS in which we build a rover based on the mars rover to competete at a competition The rover drives around a simulated mars surface and takes photos of QR codes and uploads them to a ground station 30m away

Engineering Challenges

1. The Rover must to be able to drive for over 60 meters
2. The Rover must be wirelessly controlable from at least 30 meters away
3. The Camera must be able to manuvuer and take photos

Technologies Used

  • ESP32
  • Servo motor control
  • Radio

Results & Performance

An inital rover has been manufactured and can drive.
Plans for development are underway

Fintennas

Overview

Use copper deposits on the fins of a rocket as an antenna
Use MATLAB and new research to create a flat copper antenna and manufacture fins made to this spec

Engineering Challenges

Gain needs to be -5dB minimum on 868 MHz and 2.4 GHz
Needs to be able to connect to firefly ultra in the rocket
Fins must be strong enough to withstand testing (and ideally a harder imapact.)

Technologies Used

  • Altium designer
  • MATLAB

Results & Performance

Preliminary antenna designs exist.
Fins have been structurally tested and passed.
