aardvARC

Overview

Measuring altitude with a Taser
Using a taser module with a known spark gap the and knowing the dielectric breakdown voltage of air decreases with pressure. As a rocket ascends an increasing voltage is required in order to cause an arc. This allows for a measurement of pressure and therefore altitude.

Engineering Challenges

1. The arc needs to be completely isolated and not interfere with the RF present on board.
2. The taser needs to not drain the battery too fast.
3. Safety must be a priority.

Technologies Used

  • Altium designer
  • MATLAB
  • SDR

Fintennas

Overview

Use copper deposits on the fins of a rocket as an antenna
Use MATLAB and new research to create a flat copper antenna and manufacture fins made to this spec

Engineering Challenges

Gain needs to be -5dB minimum on 868 MHz and 2.4 GHz
Needs to be able to connect to firefly ultra in the rocket
Fins must be strong enough to withstand testing (and ideally a harder imapact.)

Technologies Used

  • Altium designer
  • MATLAB
